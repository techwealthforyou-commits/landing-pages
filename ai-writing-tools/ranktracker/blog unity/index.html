Why Unity Became a Global Creator Standard

Unity evolved from an indie-friendly engine into one of the most widely used real-time 3D ecosystems for creators and developers.

Today, it powers mobile games, immersive XR experiences, industrial digital twins, and scalable creator monetization systems.

The Unity Asset Store ecosystem accelerates production by providing ready-to-use systems, VFX packs, UI kits, and workflow tools.
